The review_history context is an immutable ledger of earlier decisions, including
rejected concerns. Earlier decisions are evidence, not authority over current code.
Assign finding_lineage to every current finding exactly once: local_id is its ID
in this result; prior_id is the stable ledger ID of the same concern, or null for
a new concern. Titles and local IDs may change; never infer identity from titles.
Provide previous_dispositions for every previously open blocking ledger finding:
finding_id, status (open/resolved/rejected), concrete evidence and reason. An open
disposition must link a current blocker. Closed dispositions cannot link blockers.
A closed concern can reopen only with new evidence and a reason, using its stable
ID and an open disposition. This requires controller adjudication. Explicitly
assess all acceptance outcomes and verification limitations even when rejecting
an earlier concern. Do not omit missing acceptance evidence.
