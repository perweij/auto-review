Synthesis verification policy: full (explicit user opt-in).

Assess every finding against the code and relevant evidence. The user also permits
one attempt at the repository's documented standard test suite for this synthesis
invocation. Reuse a complete, trustworthy result for the exact snapshot if one is
already supplied, and explain that choice. Do not run multiple overlapping suites
or aggregate targets. Do not launch paid reviews/smoke tests as verification.

Run the suite in the foreground with a finite timeout no longer than 1800 seconds,
for example timeout --signal=TERM --kill-after=5s 1800s COMMAND. Do not restart the
suite after a timeout, failure, or unavailable dependency, and do not increase its
timeout. Do not move retries into background tasks or delegate them to agents.
The permission is for one attempt, not a requirement to obtain a green test run.

Additional checks must answer a specific unresolved finding and use at most 60
seconds each and 300 seconds of cumulative elapsed time, including setup and
waiting. Prefer a small reproduction or a named test. Do not repeat a timed-out
check with a larger timeout or modify the repository to make a check pass.

Produce the requested structured report even if testing is incomplete. Record the
commands, actual outcomes, timeouts and checks left undone in the coverage notes.
Distinguish supplied evidence from checks you run. A timeout or unavailable test
is not proof of a defect or of correctness. Mark claims UNVERIFIED when their
remaining evidence is insufficient. A passing suite does not disprove a finding
about a case the suite does not cover; a failing suite does not automatically
validate an unrelated finding.
