Synthesis verification policy: focused.

Your goal is to establish the evidence for each finding. Before running a test,
identify the finding it checks and what result would confirm or falsify it.
Prefer tracing the relevant code, a small reproduction, or an existing test
selected by name. Reuse relevant, trustworthy evidence for this exact snapshot
from the supplied reports or CI; distinguish that evidence from checks you run.

For a stateful failure/recovery claim (interruption, adoption, invalidation,
replanning, or partial publication), attempt a focused executable reproduction
when dependencies and permitted scratch are available. Check the actual orchestration
and shared error handling, not just an isolated conditional. If you use inspection
alone, explain for that finding why no executable check was run, which transitions
were traced, and what remains uncertain. A read-only source checkout alone does
not establish that tests cannot run. Do not mark a claim confirmed when the
unverified transition is essential to its alleged failure.

Do not run a repository-wide test suite, build, lint sweep, benchmark, or broad
integration bundle for general confidence. This also applies to aggregate targets
such as make check and to commands delegated to agents or background tasks.
Run focused checks in the foreground and inspect their results before continuing.

Limit each verification command to 60 seconds and all verification commands in
this synthesis invocation to 300 seconds of cumulative elapsed time, including
test setup and waiting. Use a bounded command timeout, such as
timeout --signal=TERM --kill-after=5s 60s COMMAND, shortened to the remaining
allowance. Do not restart a timed-out check with a larger timeout, broaden it to
a full suite, or repeat failing commands merely to obtain a passing result.
When a check is unavailable or times out, use the available evidence and move on.
Do not fix the repository or launch paid reviews/smoke tests as verification.

Before a check, inspect its documented dependencies and output locations. Use
already permitted temporary storage for caches/fixtures where supported. A
read-only checkout does not imply writable scratch. Do not relax sandbox policy
or install dependencies to make tests run; report the specific limitation instead.

Continue the code assessment and produce the requested structured report even
when testing is incomplete. Record the actual commands, outcomes, timeouts and
checks left undone in the coverage notes. A timeout or unavailable test is not
proof of a defect or of correctness. Mark a claim UNVERIFIED when its remaining
evidence is insufficient; do not invent a successful verification. A passing
test suite does not disprove a finding about a case that suite does not cover.
