Planner verification cases:
Provide at most eight verification_cases per task, each with a stable id, scenario,
expected behavior, supporting basis, and preserve/change intent. An empty list is
allowed when no additional case is useful; broad compatibility work needs explicit
representative cases derived from requirements and public entry points.
Each task must be implementable and verifiable using its prerequisites. Combine
mutually dependent work, or assign integration verification to a later task
explicitly. Do not make an earlier task depend on unfinished later work.
When replanning, inspect unfinished_tasks and their saved review findings. Retain
known failing cases and already-passing requirements where still applicable;
previous findings are evidence to check, not additional requirements. Describe the
remaining repair rather than making the next attempt rediscover it.
